PRIVATE BY DESIGN

Your browser is your business.

Everything stays in this Chrome profile. No analytics, accounts, cloud sync, browsing history, or blocked-attempt logs. Your password is stored as a salted verifier; website settings are local but aren’t encrypted.

Schedules follow your computer’s timezone. Overnight windows continue into the next day. Sleep, browser startup, and Chrome’s background scheduling can briefly delay transitions.

This is a self-control aid. Chrome still lets you disable or remove the extension. Other profiles and browsers aren’t covered. If you forget the password, remove and reinstall the extension to start over.

Keep a copy of your boundaries.

Export your saved groups, schedules, and timing to a local file. Backups aren’t encrypted and don’t include your password, visits, or pending changes. Importing replaces the draft for review; weaker settings still require the current cooldown. Unlock settings to continue.
